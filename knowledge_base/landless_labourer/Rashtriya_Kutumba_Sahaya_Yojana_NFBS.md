# Rashtriya Kutumba Sahaya Yojana NFBS

## Title & Overview
**Rashtriya Kutumba Sahaya Yojana (National Family Benefit Scheme - NFBS)**
A vital component of the National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP). This scheme provides immediate, one-time financial assistance to landless, Below Poverty Line (BPL) families in the event of the death of the primary breadwinner, mitigating immediate economic collapse.

## Target Audience
Bereaved BPL families, specifically landless agricultural laborers and daily wage earners, who have lost their primary earning member.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Financial Assistance:** A one-time lump sum grant of ₹20,000 paid directly to the surviving head of the household or the most eligible next of kin.
*   **Direct Transfer:** The amount is credited directly via DBT to the Aadhaar-seeded bank account of the beneficiary.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   The deceased must have been the primary breadwinner of the family.
*   The age of the deceased at the time of death must have been between 18 and 59 years.
*   The family must be classified as Below Poverty Line (BPL) and possess a valid BPL card.
*   The claim must generally be filed within one year of the breadwinner's death.

## Required Documents
*   Death Certificate of the primary breadwinner
*   Valid BPL Ration Card
*   Aadhaar Card of the surviving beneficiary
*   Age Proof of the deceased (Voter ID, Aadhaar, or Medical examiner's report)
*   Bank Passbook (Aadhaar-seeded)
*   FIR/Post-mortem report (if death was unnatural/accidental)

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-NFBS-01`: Revenue inspector verification determined the deceased was not the primary breadwinner.
*   `R-NFBS-02`: Age of the deceased at the time of death fell outside the statutory 18-59 years range.
*   `R-NFBS-03`: Household classification mismatch (APL instead of strictly BPL).
*   `R-NFBS-04`: Application filed post the statutory expiry window (usually >1 year after death without a valid delay condonation).

## Target Authority
Taluk Social Welfare Office, Revenue Department, and Gram Panchayat Panchayat Development Officer (PDO).
