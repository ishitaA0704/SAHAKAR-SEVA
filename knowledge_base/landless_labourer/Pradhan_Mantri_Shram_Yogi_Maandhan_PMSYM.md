# Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan PMSYM

## Title & Overview
**Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan (PM-SYM)**
A voluntary and contributory pension scheme established by the Central Government to ensure old-age protection and financial security for unorganized sector workers, ensuring they have a guaranteed monthly income in their twilight years.

## Target Audience
Unorganized sector workers, particularly landless agricultural workers, brick kiln workers, cobblers, rag pickers, domestic workers, washermen, and rickshaw pullers.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Assured Pension:** A guaranteed minimum monthly pension of ₹3,000 after attaining the age of 60 years.
*   **Matching Contribution:** The Central Government makes an exact matching 50% contribution to the beneficiary's pension account.
*   **Family Pension:** If the subscriber dies after the pension has commenced, the spouse is entitled to receive 50% of the pension (₹1,500/month) as family pension.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   Must be an unorganized worker.
*   Entry Age limit: Strictly between 18 and 40 years.
*   Monthly income must not exceed ₹15,000.
*   Must not be engaged in the organized sector (no EPF/NPS/ESIC membership) and must not be an income taxpayer.

## Required Documents
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Savings Bank Account / Jan Dhan Account with IFSC
*   Auto-debit Mandate Consent Form (to allow monthly deductions ranging from ₹55 to ₹200 based on entry age)
*   Mobile Number (must be active for OTP verification)

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-PMSYM-01`: Monthly auto-debit mandate failed or bounced due to insufficient funds.
*   `R-PMSYM-02`: EPF/NPS subscriber overlap detected in the central database.
*   `R-PMSYM-03`: Entry age violation; applicant's demographic data indicates age above 40 years.
*   `R-PMSYM-04`: Income tax payee footprint detected against the linked Aadhaar/PAN.

## Target Authority
Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) acting as the Pension Fund Manager, the Ministry of Labour and Employment, and local Common Service Centres (CSCs) for enrollment.
