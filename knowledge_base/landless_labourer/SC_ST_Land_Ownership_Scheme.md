# SC ST Land Ownership Scheme

## Title & Overview
**SC/ST Land Purchase Scheme (Land Ownership Scheme)**
An empowering state welfare initiative designed to elevate landless agricultural laborers from marginalized communities to the status of landowners. The scheme provides immense financial subsidies to buy arable agricultural land, fostering long-term economic independence and social dignity.

## Target Audience
Landless agricultural laborers strictly belonging to the Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) communities in Karnataka.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Financial Structure:** 50% of the land purchase cost is provided as an outright government subsidy, and the remaining 50% is arranged as a low-interest term loan from the respective Development Corporation.
*   **Land Area Limits:** Assistance is provided to purchase a minimum of 1 acre of wetland/irrigated land or 2 acres of dry land.
*   **Registration Fee Exemption:** Complete exemption from stamp duty and registration charges during the land purchase.
*   **Borewell / Irrigation Support:** Additional grants often bundled to dig borewells and install pump sets on the newly acquired land.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   Applicant must belong to the SC or ST category.
*   Must be a bona fide landless agricultural laborer.
*   Must be a resident of Karnataka.
*   Family's annual income must fall below the stipulated poverty limits (e.g., ₹2,00,000 for rural areas).
*   Age limit: 18 to 60 years.

## Required Documents
*   Valid Caste Certificate
*   Income Certificate
*   Landless Certificate (issued by the Tahsildar)
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Preliminary Agreement of Sale from the land seller
*   RTC (Pahani) and Encumbrance Certificate of the proposed land

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-SLOS-01`: RTC or family tree query reveals the applicant or an immediate family member already holds agricultural land.
*   `R-SLOS-02`: Caste certificate invalid, expired, or rejected by the Revenue Department database.
*   `R-SLOS-03`: Proposed land is embroiled in a legal dispute, lacks clear title, or violates the PTCL Act.
*   `R-SLOS-04`: Annual family income exceeds the statutory limits for the subsidy tier.

## Target Authority
Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Development Corporation (for SC) and Karnataka Maharshi Valmiki Scheduled Tribes Development Corporation (for ST), operating through district-level offices.
