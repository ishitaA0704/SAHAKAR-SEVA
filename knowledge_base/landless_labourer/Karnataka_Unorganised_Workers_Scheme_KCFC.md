# Karnataka Unorganised Workers Scheme KCFC

## Title & Overview
**Karnataka Unorganised Workers Social Security Scheme (KCFC)**
A state-level welfare initiative providing comprehensive social security coverage to informal sector workers. The scheme focuses on minimizing the financial shocks associated with accidental death, severe disability, and sudden medical emergencies among marginalized laborers.

## Target Audience
Unorganized sector workers, street vendors, domestic workers, head-load workers, mechanics, and daily wage laborers operating in the informal economy across Karnataka.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Accidental Death Insurance:** Ex-gratia payout of up to ₹5,000,000 to the registered nominee in case of the worker's accidental death.
*   **Disability Compensation:** Graded financial compensation ranging from ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000 based on the severity of the permanent disability.
*   **Funeral Expenses:** Immediate financial assistance of ₹10,000 to the bereaved family for funeral and last rites.
*   **Medical Assistance:** Cashless treatment coverage in empanelled state hospitals for specific occupational hazards.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   Must be formally registered with the Karnataka State Unorganised Workers Social Security Board (KSUWSSB).
*   Age criteria: Must be between 18 and 59 years at the time of enrollment.
*   Must be a resident of Karnataka.
*   Must not be covered under statutory social security schemes like the Employees' Provident Fund (EPF) or Employees' State Insurance (ESI).

## Required Documents
*   Unorganized Worker ID Card (issued by KSUWSSB)
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Bank Passbook (of the worker or the registered nominee)
*   FIR and Post-mortem report (strictly required for accidental death/insurance claims)
*   Disability Certificate issued by a District Medical Board (for disability claims)

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-KUW-01`: Worker identified in the central database as an active EPF/ESI subscriber.
*   `R-KUW-02`: Age limit exceeded; applicant is over 59 years and ineligible for new insurance coverage.
*   `R-KUW-03`: Occupational classification mismatch; applicant does not fall under the recognized 137 informal sector categories.
*   `R-KUW-04`: Medico-legal documentation incomplete (FIR or post-mortem report missing for death claim).

## Target Authority
Karnataka State Unorganised Workers Social Security Board (KSUWSSB) and the State Labour Department.
