# Mukhyamantri Sandhya Suraksha Yojana

## Title & Overview
**Mukhyamantri Sandhya Suraksha Yojana (MSSY)**
A key social welfare pension scheme initiated by the Government of Karnataka to provide social and financial security to elderly citizens, specifically the landless poor and agricultural laborers who lack a regular source of income in their twilight years.

## Target Audience
Senior citizens, elderly landless poor, marginalized farmers, and rural laborers without formal pensions or regular earning capacity.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Monthly Pension:** A steady financial assistance of ₹1,000 per month.
*   **Direct Bank Transfer:** The pension is remitted directly to the beneficiary's bank or post office account every month via DBT.
*   **Free Bus Passes:** Eligible beneficiaries often qualify for concessional or free state transport bus passes.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   Applicant must be 65 years of age or older.
*   Must be a resident of Karnataka for at least 3 consecutive years.
*   The combined annual income of the applicant and their spouse must not exceed ₹20,000.
*   The applicant must not be receiving any other statutory pension (such as Widow Pension, Disability Pension, or EPF pension).
*   The value of bank deposits held by the applicant should not exceed ₹10,000.

## Required Documents
*   Age Proof (Voter ID, Aadhaar, School Leaving Certificate, or Medical Officer's certification)
*   Income Certificate (issued by the Tahsildar)
*   Domicile/Residence Certificate
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Bank Passbook or Post Office Savings Account details
*   Two passport-size photographs

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-MSSY-01`: Cross-verification shows applicant is already receiving a Destitute Widow Pension or National Old Age Pension (NOAPS).
*   `R-MSSY-02`: Certified annual income exceeds the strict ₹20,000 ceiling.
*   `R-MSSY-03`: Age verification failed; demographic data places applicant under 65 years of age.
*   `R-MSSY-04`: Karnataka domicile verification failed (less than 3 years of residency proven).

## Target Authority
Revenue Department (Tahsildar), Gram Panchayats, and the Directorate of Social Security and Pensions, Government of Karnataka.
