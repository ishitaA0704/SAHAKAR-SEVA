# e-Shram PMSBY Integration

## Title & Overview
**e-Shram and Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) Integration**
A national integration initiative that links the e-Shram unorganized worker database directly with PMSBY. It guarantees a universal safety net against accidental fatalities and severe disabilities for vulnerable informal workers by leveraging digital enrollment.

## Target Audience
Registered unorganized laborers, construction workers, migrant workers, agricultural laborers, and platform workers across India.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Accidental Death Cover:** Payout of ₹2,00,000 to the nominee in case of the worker's death due to an accident.
*   **Permanent Total Disability Cover:** Payout of ₹2,00,000 for total loss of both eyes, both hands/feet, or one eye and one hand/foot due to an accident.
*   **Permanent Partial Disability Cover:** Payout of ₹1,00,000 for the total and irrecoverable loss of sight in one eye or loss of use of one hand or foot.
*   **Premium Waiver:** The first year's premium (₹20) is typically waived/subsidized by the Ministry of Labour and Employment upon successful e-Shram enrollment.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   Must be successfully registered on the National Database of Unorganized Workers (NDUW) / e-Shram portal.
*   Age limit: 18 to 59 years.
*   Must possess an active, Aadhaar-seeded Savings Bank Account.
*   Must not be a member of EPFO/ESIC or be a government employee.

## Required Documents
*   e-Shram Card (12-digit Universal Account Number - UAN)
*   Aadhaar Card (demographics must exactly match e-Shram)
*   Bank Account Details (with IFSC)
*   Active Mobile Number (for OTP and UAN linking)
*   Accident/FIR details and Hospital Certificates (during the claims process)

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-ESHR-01`: Exact name mismatch between the e-Shram database, Aadhaar, and Bank Account records.
*   `R-ESHR-02`: E-KYC biometric authentication pending or incomplete on the e-Shram portal.
*   `R-ESHR-03`: Pre-existing active PMSBY policy overlap via the beneficiary's home bank branch.
*   `R-ESHR-04`: Applicant flagged as an active formal sector employee in the EPFO database.

## Target Authority
Ministry of Labour & Employment (MoLE), Common Service Centres (CSCs), and participating Public Sector General Insurance Companies.
