# MGNREGA Wage Employment

## Title & Overview
**Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) - Wage Employment**
A central government employment guarantee scheme aimed at enhancing the livelihood security of people in rural areas by guaranteeing 100 days of wage-employment in a financial year to a rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work.

## Target Audience
Rural adult members willing to do unskilled manual work, specifically targeting landless agricultural laborers, marginal farmers, and rural unemployed populations.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Guaranteed Employment:** 100 days of assured wage employment per household per financial year.
*   **Notified Wage Rate:** Wages paid strictly as per the state-notified rate (e.g., ₹349 per day for Karnataka, subject to annual revision).
*   **Unemployment Allowance:** Paid if employment is not provided within 15 days of submitting a work application.
*   **Worksite Facilities:** Provision of drinking water, first aid, and creche facilities (if >5 children under age 6 are present).

## Eligibility Criteria
*   Must be a citizen of India residing in a rural area.
*   Applicant must be at least 18 years of age at the time of application.
*   Must volunteer for unskilled manual labor.
*   The household must be registered with the local Gram Panchayat.

## Required Documents
*   MGNREGA Job Card (issued by Gram Panchayat)
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook or Post Office Account (for DBT mapping)
*   Passport-size photographs (for initial registration)

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-NREGA-01`: Job card suspended, inactive, or permanently deleted from the Panchayat registry.
*   `R-FTO-04`: Bank account not mapped for NPCI/DBT, causing Fund Transfer Order (FTO) failure.
*   `R-NREGA-03`: Aadhaar Based Payment System (ABPS) demographic mismatch or biometric failure.
*   `R-NREGA-04`: 100 days statutory employment limit for the household already exhausted for the current financial year.

## Target Authority
Gram Panchayat Panchayat Development Officer (PDO), Zilla Panchayat, and the Ministry of Rural Development.
