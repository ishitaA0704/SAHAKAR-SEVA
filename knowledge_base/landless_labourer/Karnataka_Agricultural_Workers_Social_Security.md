# Karnataka Agricultural Workers Social Security

## Title & Overview
**Karnataka Agricultural Workers Social Security Scheme**
A comprehensive state-sponsored social security framework intended to provide a robust safety net for vulnerable landless farm laborers. The scheme shields laborers against occupational hazards, loss of livelihood, and extreme poverty in old age.

## Target Audience
Landless agricultural laborers, daily wage farm workers, and rural unorganized agricultural sector participants in Karnataka.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Accident Insurance:** Coverage of ₹2,00,000 for accidental death or permanent partial disability, and up to ₹4,00,000 for permanent total disability on the job.
*   **Maternity Benefit:** Financial assistance of ₹10,000 for female agricultural laborers (applicable for up to two live births).
*   **Old Age Pension:** Assured pension of ₹3,000 per month for registered landless farm laborers upon crossing 60 years of age.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   Must be an active agricultural laborer and a resident of Karnataka.
*   **Landless Status:** Must not own any agricultural land (certified by the Revenue Department).
*   **Age Limits:** 18 to 60 years for active benefits (insurance/maternity); strictly above 60 years for the pension component.
*   Must not be a beneficiary of other formal sector EPF/ESI or parallel state pensions.

## Required Documents
*   Landless Certificate (Issued by Tahsildar/Revenue Inspector)
*   BPL Ration Card
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Age Proof (Birth Certificate or Voter ID)
*   Medical/Hospitalization certificates (for maternity/insurance claims)

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-KAW-01`: RTC (Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops) query reveals applicant or spouse owns agricultural land.
*   `R-KAW-02`: Household income exceeds the established BPL threshold as per the food civil supplies database.
*   `R-KAW-03`: Age criteria violation for pension (Applicant is under 60 years of age).
*   `R-KAW-04`: Maternity benefit limit exhausted (Claim submitted for third or subsequent child).

## Target Authority
Labour Department of Karnataka, in collaboration with the local Tahsildar/Revenue Department for landless certification.
