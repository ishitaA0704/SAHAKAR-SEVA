# Ambedkar Karmika Sahaya Yojana

## Title & Overview
**Ambedkar Karmika Sahaya Yojana**
A multi-faceted welfare and upliftment scheme deployed by the Karnataka State Labour Welfare Board. It aims to improve the living conditions of unorganized and construction laborers by providing housing assistance, healthcare cover, and educational support for their children.

## Target Audience
Registered unorganized laborers, building and other construction workers (BOCW), and daily wage earners in the informal sector, along with their dependent family members.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Housing Assistance:** Financial grants up to ₹2,00,000 for the construction of a new house, or up to ₹50,000 for the repair and renovation of an existing kutcha house.
*   **Educational Scholarships:** Annual scholarships ranging from ₹2,000 to ₹20,000 for the children of registered workers, covering primary education up to professional degrees (Medical/Engineering).
*   **Maternity and Marriage Assistance:** Financial aid for the marriage of the worker's children and supplementary maternity benefits.
*   **Healthcare Cover:** Reimbursement of medical expenses for major surgeries and occupational diseases not covered under standard free health schemes.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   The worker must be actively registered with the Karnataka Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (KBOCWWB) or the Unorganized Workers Board.
*   Must have maintained an active membership for at least 1-3 years (depending on the specific benefit claimed).
*   For housing assistance, the worker must not own a pucca house in their name or their spouse's name.
*   For scholarships, the child must have passed the previous academic year with a minimum specified attendance.

## Required Documents
*   Active Labour Identity Card / KBOCW Registration Card
*   Aadhaar Card (Worker and Dependents)
*   Bank Passbook
*   School/College Fee Receipt and Marks Card (for educational scholarships)
*   Land Title / Site dimensions deed (for housing assistance)

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-AKSY-01`: Labour registration is inactive, or the annual membership renewal fee has not been paid.
*   `R-AKSY-02`: Scholarship claim duplicated; child is already receiving a major scholarship under the Social Welfare Department.
*   `R-AKSY-03`: Housing assistance rejected; records indicate the applicant previously claimed housing aid under PMAY or Basava Vasathi Yojana.
*   `R-AKSY-04`: Insufficient active membership duration (e.g., claiming marriage assistance with less than 3 years of board membership).

## Target Authority
Karnataka Labour Welfare Board and the Karnataka Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (KBOCWWB), under the Labour Department.
