# Karnataka Mathrushree Scheme

## Title & Overview
**Karnataka Mathrushree Scheme**
A targeted maternal health and nutrition scheme aimed at providing financial security to pregnant and lactating women. The scheme offsets wage loss incurred during pregnancy and childbirth for landless and BPL women, ensuring better nutritional intake and safer institutional deliveries.

## Target Audience
Pregnant and lactating women belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) households, landless laborers, and marginalized rural families.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Maternity Benefit:** Total financial assistance of ₹6,000 per pregnancy.
*   **Staggered Payouts:** 
    * ₹3,000 is transferred during the advanced stages of pregnancy (typically the 7th month) to support nutritional needs.
    * ₹3,000 is transferred post-delivery, contingent upon an institutional delivery and completion of the initial immunization cycle for the newborn.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   The beneficiary must belong to a verified BPL family.
*   The mother must be 18 years of age or older at the time of pregnancy.
*   Benefits are restricted to the first two live births only.
*   Pregnancy must be registered at an Anganwadi center or a government health facility within the first trimester.
*   Institutional delivery in a government or recognized private hospital is mandatory for the second installment.

## Required Documents
*   BPL Ration Card
*   Thayi Card (Mother and Child Protection Card) showing ANC (Ante-Natal Care) registration
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Bank Passbook (Aadhaar seeded)
*   Birth Certificate of the newborn and Immunization records (for the post-delivery installment)

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-KMS-01`: ANC (Ante-Natal Care) registration not found or registered past the stipulated trimester deadline in the Thayi portal.
*   `R-KMS-02`: Third child/pregnancy exclusion triggered (Applicant has already claimed benefits for two previous live births).
*   `R-KMS-03`: DBT failure; bank account inactive or name mismatch with the Thayi Card registry.
*   `R-KMS-04`: Delivery reported as 'Home Delivery', rendering the second installment ineligible.

## Target Authority
Department of Women and Child Development (WCD), implemented locally by Anganwadi Workers, ASHA workers, and the Child Development Project Officer (CDPO).
