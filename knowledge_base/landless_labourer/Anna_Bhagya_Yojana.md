# Anna Bhagya Yojana

## Title & Overview
**Anna Bhagya Yojana**
The flagship food security and anti-hunger program by the Government of Karnataka. It ensures zero starvation among marginalized communities by providing free food grains, securing the nutritional well-being of landless laborers and the extreme poor.

## Target Audience
Below Poverty Line (BPL) households and Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) beneficiaries, primarily consisting of landless agricultural workers and daily wage laborers.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Food Grains Allocation:** Provision of 10 Kg of free rice/food grains per person, per month for BPL and AAY cardholders.
*   **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT):** In instances of food grain shortages, the state provides a direct cash transfer of ₹170 per person per month (calculated at ₹34 per kg for a 5kg shortfall) in lieu of the physical grains.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   The household must possess a valid BPL (Below Poverty Line) or AAY (Antyodaya) ration card issued by the Karnataka Government.
*   The family members must not hold government employment, pay income tax, or own a four-wheeler.
*   All household members listed on the ration card must have their Aadhaar numbers seeded and E-KYC completed.

## Required Documents
*   Valid BPL or AAY Ration Card
*   Aadhaar Card of all family members
*   Aadhaar-seeded Bank Passbook of the Head of the Household (HoH) for DBT routing
*   Mobile Number linked to Aadhaar

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-ABY-01`: Aadhaar not seeded with the Ration Card member list.
*   `R-ABY-02`: E-KYC bio-authentication failed or is pending at the local Fair Price Shop (FPS).
*   `R-ABY-03`: System detected an APL (Above Poverty Line) classification instead of BPL/AAY.
*   `R-ABY-04`: DBT failed due to the Head of Household's bank account being frozen or lacking NPCI mapping.

## Target Authority
Department of Food, Civil Supplies & Consumer Affairs, Government of Karnataka, operating via Fair Price Shops (FPS) and Gram Panchayats.
