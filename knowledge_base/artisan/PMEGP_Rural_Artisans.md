# PMEGP Rural Artisans

## Title & Overview
**Prime Minister’s Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP) - Rural Artisans Component**
A major credit-linked subsidy program aimed at generating employment opportunities in rural and urban areas by setting up micro-enterprises and artisan units. It seeks to arrest the migration of rural youth to urban areas by enabling self-employment in traditional sectors.

## Target Audience
Rural artisans, traditional craftsmen, self-help groups (SHGs), and micro-entrepreneurs aiming to establish manufacturing or service units.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Margin Money Subsidy (Rural):** 25% subsidy for General Category; 35% subsidy for Special Categories (SC/ST, OBC, Minorities, Women, Ex-servicemen, Physically Handicapped, NER).
*   **Maximum Project Cost:** Up to ₹50 Lakhs for the manufacturing sector and up to ₹20 Lakhs for the service sector.
*   **Beneficiary Contribution:** Only 10% of project cost for General Category, and 5% for Special Categories.
*   **Bank Finance:** Remaining 65-75% of the project cost is provided as term loans by participating banks.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   Any individual above 18 years of age.
*   At least VIII standard pass for projects costing above ₹10 Lakhs in the manufacturing sector and above ₹5 Lakhs in the business/service sector.
*   Only new artisan projects/units are considered for sanction.
*   Institutions registered under the Societies Registration Act, Production Co-operative Societies, and Charitable Trusts are also eligible.

## Required Documents
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Project Report / Detailed Business Plan
*   Educational Qualification Certificates (8th Pass mark sheet, if applicable)
*   Caste/Special Category Certificate (for 35% subsidy claim)
*   Rural Area Certificate (issued by local authority/Panchayat)

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-PMEGP-01`: Proposed project is for an existing unit rather than a new micro-enterprise setup.
*   `R-PMEGP-02`: Applicant failed to meet the educational requirement for high-value projects (>₹10 Lakh).
*   `R-PMEGP-03`: Project classification error (Trade falls under the negative list of PMEGP).
*   `R-PMEGP-04`: Bank loan component rejected due to insufficient creditworthiness or lack of technical viability in the Project Report.

## Target Authority
Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC) at the national level. At the state level, implemented through State KVIC Directorates, State Khadi and Village Industries Boards (KVIBs), District Industries Centres (DICs), and banks.
