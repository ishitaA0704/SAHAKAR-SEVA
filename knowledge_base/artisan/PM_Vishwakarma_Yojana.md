# PM Vishwakarma Yojana

## Title & Overview
**PM Vishwakarma Yojana** 
A Central Sector Scheme aimed at providing end-to-end, holistic support to traditional artisans and craftspeople. The scheme seeks to improve the quality, scale, and reach of their products, integrating them with the domestic and global value chains.

## Target Audience
Traditional artisans and craftspeople engaged in 18 recognized trades, including Carpenters, Boat Makers, Armourers, Blacksmiths, Hammer and Tool Kit Makers, Locksmiths, Goldsmiths, Potters, Sculptors/Stone Carvers, Cobblers (Charmkar), Masons, Basket/Mat/Broom Makers/Coir Weavers, Doll & Toy Makers (Traditional), Barbers, Garland Makers, Washermen, Tailors, and Fishing Net Makers.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Recognition:** PM Vishwakarma Certificate and ID Card.
*   **Skill Upgradation:** Basic training of 5-7 days and advanced training of 15+ days with a stipend of ₹500 per day.
*   **Toolkit Incentive:** e-Voucher or Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) up to ₹15,000 for purchasing specialized tools.
*   **Credit Support:** Collateral-free enterprise development loans up to ₹3,00,000 (₹1 Lakh in Tranche 1, and ₹2 Lakh in Tranche 2) at a concessional interest rate of 5%, with an interest subvention cap of 8% paid by the Ministry of MSME.
*   **Digital Transaction Incentive:** ₹1 per digital transaction, up to 100 transactions monthly.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   An artisan or craftsperson working with hands and tools in one of the 18 family-based traditional trades.
*   Must be 18 years or older on the date of registration.
*   Only one member of a family (husband, wife, and unmarried children) is eligible.
*   Must not have availed loans under similar credit-based schemes (e.g., PMEGP, PM SVANidhi, Mudra) in the past 5 years.

## Required Documents
*   Aadhaar Card (must be linked to mobile number)
*   Bank Passbook / Account Details
*   Ration Card (for family verification)
*   Caste Certificate (if applicable for targeted demographic tracking)

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-PMVY-01`: Trade not officially recognized within the 18 statutory categories.
*   `R-PMVY-02`: Aadhaar demographic mismatch or mobile number unverified.
*   `R-PMVY-03`: Concurrent active loan found under PMEGP or PM SVANidhi.
*   `R-PMVY-04`: Multiple family members detected under a single Ration Card application.

## Target Authority
Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME), Central Government, in coordination with local Common Service Centres (CSCs) and Gram Panchayats.
