# Ambedkar Valmiki Vruthi Abhivruddhi

## Title & Overview
**Ambedkar-Valmiki Vruthi Abhivruddhi Scheme**
A targeted intervention by the Government of Karnataka to empower Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) artisans. The scheme aims to uplift marginalized communities by providing the necessary financial backing and modern tools to sustain and grow their traditional vocations.

## Target Audience
Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) artisans residing in Karnataka, practicing traditional crafts, weaving, and rural trades.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Financial Assistance:** Working capital grants up to ₹50,000 for purchasing raw materials and upgrading micro-units.
*   **Toolkit Subsidies:** 100% grant for the procurement of modern, trade-specific toolkits and small machinery to improve productivity.
*   **Skill Development:** Free capacity-building workshops and technical training sessions with a daily stipend.
*   **Marketing Support:** Assistance in participating in state-sponsored exhibitions (Khadi Utsavs, Handloom Expos) with TA/DA coverage.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   Must belong strictly to the Scheduled Caste (SC) or Scheduled Tribe (ST) categories.
*   Must be a permanent resident of Karnataka.
*   Family's annual income must not exceed ₹3,00,000.
*   Must be engaged in a recognized traditional artisan trade or handicraft.

## Required Documents
*   Valid Caste Certificate (issued by the Tahsildar)
*   Income Certificate
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Bank Passbook (for direct fund transfer)
*   Recent Passport Size Photographs
*   Endorsement from local Gram Panchayat or relevant artisan cooperative.

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-AVVA-01`: Caste certificate validation failed (Applicant does not belong to SC/ST category).
*   `R-AVVA-02`: Annual family income exceeds the ₹3.00 Lakh statutory cap.
*   `R-AVVA-03`: Trade not recognized as a traditional artisan vocation under scheme bylaws.
*   `R-AVVA-04`: Duplicate subsidy claimed; applicant has already availed toolkit benefits under a parallel state scheme.

## Target Authority
Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Development Corporation and Karnataka Maharshi Valmiki Scheduled Tribes Development Corporation, in coordination with the District Industries Centre (DIC).
