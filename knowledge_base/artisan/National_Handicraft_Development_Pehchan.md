# National Handicraft Development Pehchan

## Title & Overview
**National Handicraft Development Programme (NHDP) - Pehchan Initiative**
A comprehensive developmental initiative by the Ministry of Textiles focusing on the empowerment of handicraft artisans. The cornerstone of the scheme is the issuance of the 'Pehchan' ID card, which acts as a master gateway for artisans to access various central subsidies, skill upgrades, and market linkage programs.

## Target Audience
Handicraft artisans, sculptors, toy makers, and craftsmen producing traditional Indian handicrafts.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Identity & Recognition:** Issuance of the Aadhaar-linked 'Pehchan' Artisan Identity Card.
*   **Toolkit Distribution:** Free advanced toolkits and equipment to registered artisans to improve product quality.
*   **Skill Upgradation:** Sponsored participation in design and technical development workshops.
*   **Marketing Support:** TA/DA (Travelling and Dearness Allowance) and free stall space for artisans attending national and international craft exhibitions (Dilli Haat, Surajkund, etc.).
*   **Insurance Cover:** Enrollment into the Aam Admi Bima Yojana (AABY) for life and disability insurance coverage.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   Must be a practicing handicraft artisan.
*   Must be physically verified by the regional Handicrafts Service Centre (HSC) or relevant empanelled agency.
*   No strict income cap, but preference is given to BPL, SC/ST, and women artisans.
*   Age limit: Minimum 18 years for full benefits.

## Required Documents
*   Aadhaar Card (mandatory for Pehchan linking)
*   Two passport-size photographs
*   Sample of the craft/handicraft produced (for physical verification)
*   Bank Passbook
*   Existing craft certificates or state awards (if any)

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-NHDP-01`: Physical verification failed; applicant is a trader, not a primary artisan.
*   `R-NHDP-02`: Aadhaar duplication; 'Pehchan' card already issued against this Aadhaar number.
*   `R-NHDP-03`: Craft category does not align with the recognized list of Indian handicrafts.
*   `R-NHDP-04`: Bank details mismatch preventing the processing of TA/DA exhibition claims.

## Target Authority
Development Commissioner (Handicrafts), Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, operating through regional Handicrafts Service Centres (HSCs).
