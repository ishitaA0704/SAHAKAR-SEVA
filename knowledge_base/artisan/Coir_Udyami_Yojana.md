# Coir Udyami Yojana

## Title & Overview
**Coir Udyami Yojana (CUY)**
A credit-linked subsidy scheme implemented by the Coir Board to facilitate the establishment of coir units. Highly relevant for coastal and coconut-growing rural belts of Karnataka, the scheme aims to promote the coir industry, create rural employment, and empower traditional coir artisans and self-help groups.

## Target Audience
Individuals, Self Help Groups (SHGs), Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs), and Co-operative Societies located in coconut-producing rural areas aiming to set up coir-based manufacturing units (e.g., coir yarn, mats, pith blocks).

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Capital Subsidy:** 40% of the project cost is provided as a central subsidy.
*   **Bank Credit:** 55% of the project cost is provided as a term loan by participating commercial and regional banks.
*   **Beneficiary Contribution:** The artisan/entrepreneur is required to invest only 5% of the total project cost as margin money.
*   **Maximum Project Cost:** The scheme covers projects costing up to ₹10 Lakhs (plus one working capital cycle).
*   **Marketing Assistance:** Support for participation in domestic and international coir expos.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   Any individual artisan above 18 years of age.
*   Projects must exclusively be coir-based processing or manufacturing units.
*   Applicants must possess or lease land/shed suitable for setting up the coir unit.
*   There is no income ceiling for claiming assistance.
*   Must undergo entrepreneurship development training offered by the Coir Board (mandatory prior to loan disbursement).

## Required Documents
*   Aadhaar Card and PAN Card
*   Detailed Project Report (DPR) detailing machinery, raw material, and ROI.
*   Title Deed / Lease Agreement for the industrial shed or land.
*   Quotations for coir machinery from certified vendors.
*   Coir Board Training Certificate (EDP certificate).

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-CUY-01`: Proposed project scope includes non-coir processing (e.g., generic agriculture), violating scheme mandate.
*   `R-CUY-02`: Applicant failed to complete the mandatory Entrepreneurship Development Programme (EDP) training.
*   `R-CUY-03`: Property documents (Lease/Deed) unverified or lacking industrial use clearance.
*   `R-CUY-04`: Beneficiary contribution of 5% margin money not deposited in the linked loan account.

## Target Authority
Coir Board (Ministry of MSME, Government of India), implemented through Coir Board Regional Offices, Sub-Regional Offices, and District Industries Centres (DICs) in collaboration with Scheduled Commercial Banks.
