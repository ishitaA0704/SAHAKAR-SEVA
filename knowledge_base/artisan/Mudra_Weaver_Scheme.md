# Mudra Weaver Scheme

## Title & Overview
**Mudra Weaver Scheme (Weaver MUDRA Scheme)**
A specialized component of the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) tailored specifically for the handloom sector. It aims to provide adequate and timely credit (working capital and term loans) to weavers to support their operational needs and technological upgradation.

## Target Audience
Handloom weavers, weaver entrepreneurs, and weavers belonging to Self Help Groups (SHGs) or Joint Liability Groups (JLGs).

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Credit Provision:** Concessional loans up to ₹50,000 (Shishu), ₹5,00,000 (Kishore), and up to ₹10,00,000 (Tarun).
*   **Margin Money Assistance:** 20% of the project cost (maximum of ₹10,000) is provided as margin money subsidy to handloom weavers.
*   **Interest Subvention:** Interest subvention of 6% for a period of three years, ensuring the effective interest rate remains affordable.
*   **Credit Guarantee:** Loans are covered under the Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE), removing collateral requirements.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   Existing handloom weavers and allied workers possessing an identity card issued by the Ministry of Textiles or State Government.
*   Weavers should have a clean credit track record (no NPA).
*   Must possess a viable business plan for working capital utilization or loom upgradation.
*   Age limit: 18 years and above.

## Required Documents
*   Weaver Identity Card (Pehchan Card / State ID)
*   Aadhaar Card and PAN Card
*   Bank Statements (last 6 months)
*   Quotation for machinery/yarn (if applying for Kishore/Tarun category)
*   Proof of SC/ST/OBC category (if claiming priority sector benefits)

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-MWS-01`: Weaver ID not verifiable in the central handloom database.
*   `R-MWS-02`: Applicant currently holds a Non-Performing Asset (NPA) status with a commercial bank.
*   `R-MWS-03`: Margin money component calculation error or mismatch in project report.
*   `R-MWS-04`: Requested loan amount exceeds the Tarun category limit (₹10 Lakhs).

## Target Authority
Ministry of Textiles (Development Commissioner for Handlooms), disbursed through Scheduled Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks, and State Cooperative Banks.
