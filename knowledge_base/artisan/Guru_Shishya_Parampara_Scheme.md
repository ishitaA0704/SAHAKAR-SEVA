# Guru Shishya Parampara Scheme

## Title & Overview
**Guru Shishya Parampara Scheme**
A unique cultural preservation and skill transfer scheme aimed at keeping fading traditional arts and crafts alive. The scheme supports master craftspeople (Gurus) in transmitting their specialized, heritage skills to the younger generation (Shishyas) through an intensive, hands-on apprenticeship model.

## Target Audience
Master craftspeople (National/State awardees) acting as Gurus, and rural youth/artisan trainees acting as Shishyas. Focus is on endangered and highly specialized traditional crafts.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Guru Honorarium:** Master craftspersons receive a monthly honorarium of ₹7,500 to ₹10,000 to compensate for their time and material usage.
*   **Shishya Stipend:** Each trainee (Shishya) receives a monthly stipend of ₹3,000 to support them during the training period (usually 6 to 12 months).
*   **Material Allowance:** A fixed grant is provided per batch to cover the cost of raw materials utilized during the training.
*   **Certification:** Trainees receive a recognized certificate from the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts) upon successful completion.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   **For Gurus:** Must be a National Awardee, State Awardee, Shilp Guru, or a master craftsperson with over 10 years of documented experience in a rare craft.
*   **For Shishyas:** Age must be between 15 and 35 years. Must demonstrate a genuine interest in the craft; preference given to children of practicing artisans.
*   A single Guru can train a maximum of 5 to 10 Shishyas per sanctioned batch.

## Required Documents
*   Award Certificates / Experience Letters (for Gurus)
*   Aadhaar Card (for both Guru and Shishyas)
*   Bank Passbooks (mandatory for direct stipend/honorarium routing)
*   Age Proof / Birth Certificate (for Shishyas)
*   Training Proposal / Curriculum submitted to the Regional Director.

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-GSP-01`: Proposed craft does not fall under the 'endangered' or 'traditional heritage' classification.
*   `R-GSP-02`: Guru's credentials fail verification (Not an awardee or lacking 10 years of formal recognition).
*   `R-GSP-03`: Shishya age violation (Trainee age falls outside the 15-35 years bracket).
*   `R-GSP-04`: Bank accounts of Shishyas are not Aadhaar-seeded, leading to stipend remittance failure.

## Target Authority
Development Commissioner (Handicrafts), Ministry of Textiles, Government of India, managed via Regional Directors and local Handicrafts Service Centres (HSCs).
