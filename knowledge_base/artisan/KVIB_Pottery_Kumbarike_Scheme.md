# KVIB Pottery Kumbarike Scheme

## Title & Overview
**KVIB Pottery (Kumbarike) Scheme**
A localized intervention by the Karnataka State Khadi and Village Industries Board (KVIB) designed to modernize the traditional pottery sector. The scheme focuses on drudgery reduction, productivity enhancement, and income augmentation for traditional potters.

## Target Audience
Traditional rural potters (Kumbara community) and rural clay artisans residing in Karnataka.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Equipment Subsidy:** 100% grant/subsidy on the procurement of modernized electric pottery wheels (Shaila wheels).
*   **Infrastructure Support:** Distribution of pug mills (clay mixing machines) at subsidized rates (up to 75% subsidy) to community groups.
*   **Working Capital:** Small-scale financial grants (up to ₹10,000) for bulk procurement of raw clay and firewood/fuel for kilns.
*   **Skill Development:** Free technical training on operating electric wheels and manufacturing diversified terracotta products.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   Must be a traditional potter by profession, recognized by the local Gram Panchayat.
*   Must be a resident of Karnataka.
*   Applicant age must be between 18 and 55 years.
*   Preference is given to women artisans and those belonging to SC/ST demographics.

## Required Documents
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Caste/Community Certificate (to verify Kumbara/Potter demographic or SC/ST status)
*   Gram Panchayat Endorsement / Recommendation Letter
*   Bank Passbook for DBT of any working capital grants
*   Passport-size photographs

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-KVIB-01`: Gram Panchayat endorsement missing or unverified.
*   `R-KVIB-02`: Applicant has already received an electric pottery wheel under previous KVIB phases.
*   `R-KVIB-03`: Age criteria violation (Applicant over 55 years of age).
*   `R-KVIB-04`: Misclassification of trade (Applicant not engaged in traditional pottery/terracotta work).

## Target Authority
Karnataka State Khadi and Village Industries Board (KVIB), under the Department of Commerce and Industries, Government of Karnataka.
