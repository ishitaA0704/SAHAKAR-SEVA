# Artisan Credit Card (ACC)

## Title & Overview
**Artisan Credit Card (ACC)**
A specialized credit delivery mechanism aimed at providing adequate and timely working capital and term loans to artisans. The scheme mitigates the financial constraints faced by traditional craftspeople in procuring raw materials and upgrading their production infrastructure.

## Target Audience
Artisans and craftspeople registered with the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts), encompassing various traditional trades across rural and semi-urban clusters.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Credit Limit:** Working capital and term loan assistance up to ₹2,00,000.
*   **Interest Subvention:** Concessional interest rates typically subsidized by state/central MSME policies.
*   **Flexibility:** Revolving cash credit facility allowing multiple drawdowns and repayments within the sanctioned limit.
*   **Margin Money:** Minimal to nil margin money requirement for loans up to ₹50,000.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   Must be a practicing artisan possessing an Artisan Identity Card (Pehchan Card) issued by the Development Commissioner (Handicrafts).
*   Must belong to a recognized self-help group (SHG), Joint Liability Group (JLG), or apply as an individual artisan.
*   Must have a satisfactory credit history with no ongoing defaults with financial institutions.
*   Age limit: 18 to 65 years.

## Required Documents
*   Pehchan Card / Artisan Identity Card
*   Aadhaar Card and PAN Card (if applicable)
*   Project Report / Business Plan (for loans above ₹50,000)
*   Bank Passbook / Statements of the last 6 months
*   Quotations for tools/equipment (if applying for term loans)

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-ACC-01`: Development Commissioner (Handicrafts) ID not found or inactive.
*   `R-ACC-02`: Poor CIBIL score or existing Non-Performing Asset (NPA) flag in the banking system.
*   `R-ACC-03`: Proposed scale of finance exceeds maximum statutory limit of ₹2 Lakhs without adequate collateral.
*   `R-ACC-04`: Age of applicant falls outside the permissible demographic limits (18-65 years).

## Target Authority
Development Commissioner (Handicrafts), Ministry of Textiles, implemented through Scheduled Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), and District Central Cooperative Banks (DCCBs).
