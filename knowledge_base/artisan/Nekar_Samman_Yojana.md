# Nekar Samman Yojana

## Title & Overview
**Nekar Samman Yojana**
A flagship direct financial assistance scheme by the Government of Karnataka specifically designed to provide socio-economic security and immediate financial relief to traditional weavers facing distress.

## Target Audience
Registered Handloom and Powerloom weavers operating within the state of Karnataka.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Financial Assistance:** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) of ₹5,000 per annum directly credited to the beneficiary's Aadhaar-seeded bank account.
*   **Economic Relief:** Acts as a buffer against seasonal market fluctuations and raw material price volatility.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   The applicant must be a legal resident/domicile of Karnataka.
*   The applicant must be actively engaged in the handloom or powerloom weaving sector.
*   Must be formally registered with the Department of Handlooms and Textiles, Government of Karnataka.
*   Must apply through the Seva Sindhu portal within the stipulated annual window.

## Required Documents
*   Aadhaar Card
*   Weaver Registration ID / Certificate issued by the Department of Handlooms and Textiles
*   Seva Sindhu Portal Registration Receipt
*   Bank Passbook (Aadhaar seeded)
*   Ration Card or Domicile Certificate

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-NSY-01`: Weaver registration ID invalid, expired, or unregistered with the state department.
*   `R-NSY-02`: Bank account is not seeded with Aadhaar, resulting in DBT routing failure.
*   `R-NSY-03`: Applicant domicile verification failed (non-Karnataka resident).
*   `R-NSY-04`: Duplicate application detected across multiple family members on the Seva Sindhu portal.

## Target Authority
Department of Handlooms and Textiles, Government of Karnataka, facilitated via Taluk/District Panchayat Offices and the Seva Sindhu portal.
