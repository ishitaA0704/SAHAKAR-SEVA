# Suvarna Vastra Neethi

## Title & Overview
**Suvarna Vastra Neethi (Karnataka Textile Policy)**
A dedicated textile and garment policy by the Government of Karnataka aiming to comprehensively develop the textile value chain. For rural artisans and small-scale operators, it provides robust infrastructure support, power tariff concessions, and capacity building to powerloom and handloom weavers.

## Target Audience
Small-scale powerloom weavers, handloom cooperatives, and micro-textile enterprises operating within Karnataka.

## Scheme Benefits
*   **Power Tariff Concession:** Subsidy on electricity charges for micro and small powerloom units (up to 20 HP connection), significantly reducing operating costs.
*   **Credit Linked Capital Subsidy:** 15% to 20% capital subsidy on the benchmarked cost of machinery for technological upgradation (e.g., upgrading plain looms to auto-looms/rapier looms).
*   **Interest Subsidy:** 5% interest subvention on term loans taken for modernizing weaving units.
*   **Market Infrastructure:** Financial grants for establishing yarn banks and common facility centres (CFCs) in weaver clusters.

## Eligibility Criteria
*   The enterprise or weaver must be registered as a MSME with the Udyam portal.
*   Must be located within the state of Karnataka.
*   Power tariff concessions apply strictly to units using up to 20 HP sanctioned load.
*   Machinery purchased must be new and compliant with the standard benchmarks set by the Department of Textiles.

## Required Documents
*   Udyam Registration Certificate
*   Electricity Bill (showing sanctioned load and RR number)
*   Bank Term Loan Sanction Letter
*   Invoices and Quotations for new machinery
*   Aadhaar Card and PAN of the proprietor/cooperative head

## Standard Rejection Codes
*   `R-SVN-01`: Sanctioned electrical load exceeds the 20 HP statutory limit for power tariff subsidy.
*   `R-SVN-02`: Machinery invoices denote second-hand or imported used equipment, which is ineligible for capital subsidy.
*   `R-SVN-03`: Udyam registration is invalid or classifies the unit outside the textile manufacturing sector.
*   `R-SVN-04`: Term loan account is flagged as NPA, halting interest subvention claims.

## Target Authority
Department of Handlooms and Textiles, Government of Karnataka, in coordination with the local District Electricity Supply Companies (e.g., BESCOM, HESCOM, GESCOM).
