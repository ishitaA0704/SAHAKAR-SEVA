# Karnataka Raitha Vidya Nidhi

Basic Information
Official Name: Chief Minister's Raitha Vidya Nidhi Scholarship Scheme
Abbreviation: Raitha Vidya Nidhi / KRVN
Government: Karnataka State
Ministry/Department: Department of Agriculture / Department of Social Welfare / Higher Education Department, Karnataka
Implementing Agency: State Scholarship Portal (SSP Karnataka) & e-Governance Department
Current Status: Active (2026)
Official Website: https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in | https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in

Purpose
Raitha Vidya Nidhi provides financial assistance and scholarships to the children of farmers in Karnataka to encourage higher education, reduce dropout rates, and support academic expenses from Class 8/High School to Professional/Post-Graduate degrees.

Target Beneficiaries
- Students who are sons or daughters of farmers in Karnataka
- Children of landowning farmers registered in FRUITS portal

Eligibility
Eligible
- Student must be a permanent resident of Karnataka.
- Father or mother of student must be a registered farmer with a valid FRUITS ID (FID) and landholding record in Karnataka.
- Student must be enrolled in an officially recognized institution in Karnataka (Class 8 to 10 for girls; Class 11, 12, ITI, Diploma, BA, BSc, BCom, MBBS, BE/BTech, Post-Graduation for all).
- Student must have passed the previous academic year.

Not Eligible
- Students whose parents are not registered farmers in Karnataka FRUITS database.
- Students receiving full double scholarships under contradictory state schemes (though SSP auto-adjusts top-up benefits).

Benefits
| Education Level | Benefit Amount (Per Year) | Frequency / Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Class 8 to Class 10 (Girls) | ₹2,500 | Annual Direct Benefit Transfer |
| Class 11 & Class 12 (PUC / ITI) | ₹2,500 (Boys) / ₹3,000 (Girls) | Annual Direct Benefit Transfer |
| Diploma / Poly-technic | ₹4,500 (Boys) / ₹5,000 (Girls) | Annual Direct Benefit Transfer |
| Under-Graduate (BA, BCom, BSc, etc.) | ₹5,000 (Boys) / ₹5,500 (Girls) | Annual Direct Benefit Transfer |
| Professional Degrees (BE, BTech, MBBS, etc.) | ₹10,000 (Boys) / ₹11,000 (Girls) | Annual Direct Benefit Transfer |
| Post-Graduate (MA, MSc, MCom, MBA, etc.) | ₹10,000 (Boys) / ₹11,000 (Girls) | Annual Direct Benefit Transfer |

Required Documents
- Student Aadhaar Card
- Parent's Aadhaar Card & Parent's FRUITS ID (FID)
- College/School Admission Fee Receipt & Student ID
- Marksheets of previous academic year
- Bank Account Passbook (Aadhaar seeded)

Application Process
1. Online Registration on SSP:
   - Visit Karnataka State Scholarship Portal (ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in or ssp.prematric.karnataka.gov.in).
   - Register creating a Student ID using student's Aadhaar number.
2. Enter Parent's FRUITS ID:
   - Enter father's or mother's FRUITS ID (FID). System automatically verifies agricultural landholding.
3. Submit Educational Details:
   - Enter college fee receipt number and course details (auto-verified via SATS/KSEEB/University database).
4. Direct Transfer:
   - Upon verification, scholarship amount is credited directly to student's bank account via DBT.

Where to Apply
Online: https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in | https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in
Offline: School/College scholarship helpdesk, Grama One, Karnataka One counters.

Important Conditions
- Parent MUST have a valid FRUITS ID (FID). If parent doesn't have FID, parent must first register land RTC at RSK to get FID.

Farmer FAQs
Q1. Can a farmer's daughter studying in Class 9 get Raitha Vidya Nidhi?
Answer: Yes, female students from Class 8 to 10 receive ₹2,500 per year.

Q2. How much scholarship does an Engineering or Medical student get under this scheme?
Answer: Male students get ₹10,000 per year and female students get ₹11,000 per year.

Q3. Is parent's FRUITS ID compulsory?
Answer: Yes, the system verifies farmer identity using the parent's FRUITS ID (FID).

Q4. Where can students apply for Raitha Vidya Nidhi?
Answer: Online through the Karnataka State Scholarship Portal (SSP) at ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in.

Q5. Can sons of farmers also get scholarship for PUC / Diploma?
Answer: Yes, male students studying Class 11/12/ITI get ₹2,500/year and Diploma students get ₹4,500/year.
