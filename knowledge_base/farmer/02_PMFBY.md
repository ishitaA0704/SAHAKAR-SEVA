# PMFBY — Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana

Basic Information
Official Name: Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana
Abbreviation: PMFBY
Government: Central / Karnataka State (Joint Implementation)
Ministry/Department: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / Department of Agriculture, Government of Karnataka
Implementing Agency: Empaneled Insurance Companies & Agriculture Insurance Company of India (AIC)
Current Status: Active (2026)
Official Website: https://pmfby.gov.in | https://samrakshane.karnataka.gov.in

Purpose
PMFBY provides comprehensive insurance coverage to farmers against crop loss or damage resulting from non-preventable natural risks such as drought, flood, inundation, pests, diseases, landslide, cyclone, and post-harvest losses.

Target Beneficiaries
- Small farmers
- Marginal farmers
- Landowning farmers
- Tenant farmers
- Sharecroppers
- Farmers growing notified crops in notified areas/insurance units

Eligibility
Eligible
- All farmers (including sharecroppers and tenant farmers) growing notified crops in notified areas.
- Voluntary enrollment for non-loanee farmers.
- Automatic/Voluntary enrollment for loanee farmers through bank accounts.

Not Eligible
- Farmers growing non-notified crops in non-notified areas.
- Losses due to preventable risks (e.g., poor husbandry, intentional damage, theft, war, nuclear hazards).
- Subscribed applications submitted after the cutoff deadline for the crop season.

Benefits
| Benefit | Amount / Details | Frequency / Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Premium Subsidy | Farmer pays fixed maximum premium rate: 2.0% for Kharif crops, 1.5% for Rabi crops, and 5.0% for Commercial/Horticultural crops. Remaining premium is subsidized 50:50 by Central and Karnataka State Governments. | Per crop season / Per hectare |
| Claim Payout | Financial compensation up to Sum Insured for pre-sowing losses, mid-season adversity, localized calamities, post-harvest losses, and widespread crop failure based on Crop Cutting Experiments (CCE). | Per loss event / Per season |

Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card
- Land ownership document (RTC / Pahani) or Land Lease Agreement (for tenant farmers)
- Sowing Certificate / Self-Declaration of crop sown
- Bank Account Passbook
- FRUITS ID (FID)

Application Process
1. Identify Notified Crops: Check if your crop and village are notified under Samrakshane portal for the season.
2. Online Application:
   - Visit PMFBY portal (pmfby.gov.in) or Karnataka Samrakshane Portal (samrakshane.karnataka.gov.in).
   - Register/login using mobile number and FRUITS ID.
   - Select state (Karnataka), district, taluk, hobli, village, survey number, and crop sown.
   - Enter bank account details and upload RTC/Sowing certificate.
   - Pay the farmer share of premium online.
3. Offline Application:
   - Visit nearest bank branch, Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK), or Grama One center before deadline.

Where to Apply
Online: https://pmfby.gov.in | https://samrakshane.karnataka.gov.in
Offline: Banks, Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS), Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK), Grama One, CSCs.

Important Conditions
- Enrollment must be completed before season deadlines (Kharif and Rabi cutoff dates set by state government).
- Loss reporting: Localized losses (flood, landslide, hail) must be reported to insurance provider or Samrakshane portal within 72 hours of occurrence.

Farmer FAQs
Q1. Is crop insurance mandatory for farmers taking agricultural loans?
Answer: Enrollment is voluntary for all farmers, including loanee farmers. Loanee farmers must opt out in writing before the deadline if they do not wish to enroll.

Q2. How much premium does a farmer have to pay for ragi or paddy in Kharif?
Answer: Maximum 2% of the sum insured for Kharif food crops like ragi and paddy.

Q3. What if my crop is damaged by heavy rain or flood right after harvest?
Answer: Post-harvest losses for crops left in cut-and-spread condition in the field are covered up to 14 days after harvesting.

Q4. Where can I check my insurance application status in Karnataka?
Answer: You can check your status on the Karnataka Samrakshane portal (samrakshane.karnataka.gov.in) using your mobile number or proposal number.

Q5. Can tenant farmers apply for PMFBY?
Answer: Yes, tenant farmers and sharecroppers are eligible by providing land lease documents or sowing certificates.
