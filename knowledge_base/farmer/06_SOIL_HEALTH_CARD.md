# Soil Health Card Scheme

Basic Information
Official Name: Soil Health Card Scheme
Abbreviation: SHC
Government: Central / State (60:40 share)
Ministry/Department: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / Department of Agriculture, Government of Karnataka
Implementing Agency: State Agricultural Laboratories / Raitha Samparka Kendras (RSK)
Current Status: Active (2026)
Official Website: https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in | https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in

Purpose
The scheme provides farmers with crop-wise nutrient recommendations and soil fertility status reports to promote balanced fertilizer use, improve soil health, lower production costs, and maximize crop yields.

Target Beneficiaries
- All categories of landholding farmers across Karnataka
- Small, marginal, and tenant farmers

Eligibility
Eligible
- All farmers owning or cultivating agricultural land in Karnataka.

Not Eligible
- Non-agricultural landholders.

Benefits
| Benefit | Amount / Details | Frequency / Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Soil Test Report & Card | Free soil testing for 12 parameters (N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, Bo, pH, EC, OC) along with customized fertilizer/organic manure recommendations for selected crops. | Issued once every 3 years per farm holding |

Required Documents
- Land Record (RTC / Pahani) details (Survey number, Hissa number)
- Aadhaar Card / Mobile Number
- Soil sample submitted as per standard sampling guidelines

Application Process
1. Sample Collection: Soil samples are collected by agriculture department staff/GPS-tracked samplers or submitted directly by the farmer at Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK).
2. Testing: Samples are sent to designated Soil Testing Laboratories (STL) in Karnataka.
3. Card Distribution: Results are updated on the Soil Health portal and physical Soil Health Cards are distributed to farmers at RSK or downloaded online.

Where to Apply
Online: https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in
Offline: Local Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK), District Soil Testing Labs, Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVK).

Important Conditions
- Soil samples should ideally be collected post-harvest before land preparation for the next crop.

Farmer FAQs
Q1. Does a farmer need to pay for a Soil Health Card?
Answer: No, soil sampling and health card distribution under the government drive are provided free of cost.

Q2. What parameters are tested in the soil?
Answer: 12 parameters are tested: Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium, Sulphur, Zinc, Iron, Copper, Manganese, Boron, pH, Electrical Conductivity, and Organic Carbon.

Q3. How often is a Soil Health Card issued?
Answer: Cards are updated and issued once every 3 years.

Q4. Where can I submit my soil sample in Karnataka?
Answer: Submit your soil sample to your nearest Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK) or District Agricultural Office.

Q5. Can I view my Soil Health Card online?
Answer: Yes, you can view and download your card from soilhealth.dac.gov.in using your state, district, and survey number.
