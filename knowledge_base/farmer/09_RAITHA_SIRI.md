# Karnataka Raitha Siri

Basic Information
Official Name: Raitha Siri Scheme
Abbreviation: Raitha Siri
Government: Karnataka State
Ministry/Department: Department of Agriculture, Government of Karnataka
Implementing Agency: State Department of Agriculture / Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK)
Current Status: Active (2026)
Official Website: https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in | https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in

Purpose
Raitha Siri encourages the cultivation of minor millets (Siri Dhanya) such as Foxtail Millet, Little Millet, Kodo Millet, Proso Millet, and Barnyard Millet by providing direct financial incentives to farmers, promoting drought-resilient and nutrition-rich agriculture.

Target Beneficiaries
- Small and marginal farmers in Karnataka
- Rain-fed millet growers
- SC/ST farmers growing minor millets

Eligibility
Eligible
- Farmers owning agricultural land in Karnataka registered under FRUITS portal.
- Farmers actively cultivating notified minor millets (Siri Dhanya) during Kharif or Rabi season.

Not Eligible
- Farmers growing major cereals (wheat/paddy) or non-millet crops on the claimed land parcel.
- Cultivators without valid land ownership / RTC mapping on FRUITS portal.

Benefits
| Benefit | Amount / Details | Frequency / Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Millet Cultivation Financial Assistance | ₹10,000 per hectare incentive deposited directly into farmer's bank account via DBT. | Annual incentive (maximum ceiling limit up to 2 hectares per beneficiary) |

Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card
- Land Ownership Record (RTC / Pahani) showing minor millet crop entry
- FRUITS ID (FID)
- Bank Account Passbook (Aadhaar linked)
- Mobile Number

Application Process
1. Sowing & Declaration: Farmer sows minor millets and registers crop details in Karnataka Crop Survey App / FRUITS portal.
2. Application Submission: Apply via FRUITS portal or at local Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK).
3. Field Inspection: Agriculture Officer / Village Accountant verifies millet crop standing in the field.
4. DBT Payout: ₹10,000 per hectare is directly transferred to the farmer's account.

Where to Apply
Online: https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in | https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in
Offline: Local Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK), Office of Assistant Director of Agriculture (ADA).

Important Conditions
- Field verification through official Karnataka Crop Survey data is mandatory.
- Benefit is strictly restricted to notified minor millets (Siri Dhanya).

Farmer FAQs
Q1. How much financial support is given under Raitha Siri?
Answer: ₹10,000 per hectare for farmers growing minor millets in Karnataka.

Q2. Which crops are covered under Raitha Siri?
Answer: Minor millets (Siri Dhanya) including Foxtail millet (Navane), Little millet (Saame), Kodo millet (Harka), Proso millet (Baragu), and Barnyard millet (Oodalu).

Q3. Can a farmer with 2 acres of minor millets apply?
Answer: Yes. 2 acres (~0.8 hectares) will receive a proportional incentive of approximately ₹8,000.

Q4. Is FRUITS ID mandatory for Raitha Siri?
Answer: Yes, all Karnataka state agricultural subsidies require a valid FRUITS ID (FID).

Q5. How is crop cultivation verified?
Answer: Verification is done via the government's official Farmer Crop Survey application and RSK officer field visits.
