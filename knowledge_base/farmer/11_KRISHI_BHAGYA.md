# Karnataka Krishi Bhagya

Basic Information
Official Name: Krishi Bhagya Scheme
Abbreviation: Krishi Bhagya
Government: Karnataka State
Ministry/Department: Department of Agriculture, Government of Karnataka
Implementing Agency: Department of Agriculture & Department of Horticulture, Karnataka
Current Status: Active (2026)
Official Website: https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in | https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in

Purpose
Krishi Bhagya focuses on rainwater conservation and sustainable farming in rain-fed dryland regions of Karnataka by subsidizing farm ponds (Krishi Honda), polythene lining, diesel/solar pump sets, micro-irrigation systems, and shade-net structures.

Target Beneficiaries
- Small and marginal dryland farmers
- Farmers in rain-fed/drought-prone districts of Karnataka (Bayaluseeme and North Karnataka)
- SC/ST farmers in eligible zones

Eligibility
Eligible
- Landowning farmers in notified dryland taluks/districts of Karnataka.
- Farmers enrolled on the Karnataka FRUITS portal.
- SC/ST farmers receive higher subsidy percentages.

Not Eligible
- Farmers with fully canal-irrigated land parcels.
- Applicants who have already received farm pond subsidies under MGNREGA or other schemes for the same land parcel.

Benefits
| Benefit | Amount / Details | Frequency / Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Farm Pond (Krishi Honda) Subsidy | 80% subsidy for General category farmers; up to 90% subsidy for SC/ST farmers for constructing farm ponds, polythene lining, pump sets, and shade-nets. (Subsidy amount ranges from ₹70,000 to ₹85,000+ for farm pond packages). | One-time support per farmer family |

Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card
- Land Record (RTC / Pahani)
- FRUITS ID (FID)
- Bank Account Passbook
- Caste Certificate (for SC/ST higher subsidy)
- Photograph of proposed site location

Application Process
1. Application: Apply online via FRUITS / Raitamitra portal or submit application at Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK).
2. Site Inspection & Approval: Assistant Agricultural Officer (AAO) inspects location and approves farm pond dimensions.
3. Construction & Verification: Farmer constructs Krishi Honda (or gets it constructed via empaneled JCB/contractors). AAO measures dimensions, checks polythene lining, takes geotagged photos.
4. Subsidy Credit: Subsidy amount is directly transferred via DBT to farmer’s bank account.

Where to Apply
Online: https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in | https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in
Offline: Local Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK), Office of Assistant Director of Agriculture (ADA).

Important Conditions
- Farm pond size must conform to government specifications based on landholding size.
- Geotagging before and after construction is mandatory.

Farmer FAQs
Q1. How much subsidy is provided for Krishi Honda (farm pond) in Karnataka?
Answer: General category farmers get an 80% subsidy, while SC/ST farmers get up to a 90% subsidy.

Q2. Who is eligible for Krishi Bhagya?
Answer: Dryland and rainfed farmers in notified drought-prone areas of Karnataka.

Q3. Does Krishi Bhagya cover diesel pumps or tarpaulin lining?
Answer: Yes, the scheme package includes subsidies for farm ponds, polythene lining sheets, diesel/solar pumps, and drip/sprinkler systems.

Q4. Where can I submit my application for Krishi Bhagya?
Answer: Submit your application at your local Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK) or apply through the state FRUITS portal.

Q5. Can I construct a farm pond under Krishi Bhagya if I already built one under MGNREGA?
Answer: No, duplicate subsidies for the same land parcel are not permitted.
