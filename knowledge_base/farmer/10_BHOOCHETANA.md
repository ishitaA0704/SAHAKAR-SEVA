# Karnataka Bhoochetana

Basic Information
Official Name: Bhoochetana Scheme (Soil Fertility & Yield Enhancement Mission)
Abbreviation: Bhoochetana
Government: Karnataka State
Ministry/Department: Department of Agriculture, Government of Karnataka (in technical collaboration with ICRISAT)
Implementing Agency: Department of Agriculture, Karnataka / Raitha Samparka Kendras
Current Status: Active (2026 - Integrated into State Sustainable Farming & Soil Health Initiatives)
Official Website: https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in

Purpose
Bhoochetana aims to enhance productivity of dryland/rainfed agriculture across Karnataka by correcting soil nutrient deficiencies (micronutrients like Zinc, Boron, Sulphur) and promoting soil-test-based nutrient management.

Target Beneficiaries
- Dryland and rainfed farmers in Karnataka
- Small and marginal farmers
- SC/ST farmers

Eligibility
Eligible
- All dryland and rainfed farmers cultivating field crops in Karnataka.

Not Eligible
- Non-agricultural landholders.

Benefits
| Benefit | Amount / Details | Frequency / Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Subsidized Inputs & Technical Guidance | 50% subsidy on micronutrients (Gypsum, Zinc Sulphate, Borax) and bio-fertilizers. Free soil test based nutrient advisory and expert guidance. | Per crop season |

Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card
- Land Ownership Record (RTC / Pahani)
- FRUITS ID (FID)

Application Process
1. Contact RSK: Visit nearest Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK) before cropping season.
2. Submit Details: Show FRUITS ID / RTC.
3. Purchase Inputs: Collect micro-nutrients, bio-fertilizers, and seed treatment inputs at 50% subsidized price.

Where to Apply
Online: Information on https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in
Offline: Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK) in all hoblis of Karnataka.

Important Conditions
- Subsidized inputs are distributed strictly based on village soil test recommendations.

Farmer FAQs
Q1. What support does a dryland farmer get under Bhoochetana?
Answer: Farmers get 50% subsidy on essential soil micronutrients like Zinc, Boron, and Gypsum, along with bio-fertilizers.

Q2. Is Bhoochetana applicable to rain-fed crops like Ragi and Jowar?
Answer: Yes, Bhoochetana specifically targets rainfed dryland crops including ragi, jowar, pulses, and oilseeds.

Q3. Where can I buy subsidized micronutrients under this scheme?
Answer: At your local hobli Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK).

Q4. Do I need a soil test report first?
Answer: RSK officers cross-reference your village soil health grid map to prescribe the correct subsidized micronutrient packet.

Q5. What document do I need to take to RSK?
Answer: Take your Aadhaar Card, RTC/Pahani copy, or FRUITS ID card.
