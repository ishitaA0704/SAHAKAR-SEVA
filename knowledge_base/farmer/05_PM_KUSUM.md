# PM-KUSUM — Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan

Basic Information
Official Name: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan
Abbreviation: PM-KUSUM
Government: Central / Karnataka State (Joint Implementation)
Ministry/Department: Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) / Energy Department, Government of Karnataka / KREDL
Implementing Agency: Karnataka Renewable Energy Development Limited (KREDL) & Electricity Distribution Companies (BESCOM, HESCOM, MESCOM, GESCOM, CESC)
Current Status: Active (2026)
Official Website: https://pmkusum.mnre.gov.in | https://kredl.karnataka.gov.in

Purpose
PM-KUSUM provides energy security to farmers by installing standalone off-grid solar water pumps, solarizing existing grid-connected agricultural pumps, and enabling farmers to set up small solar power plants on barren/uncultivable land to generate extra income.

Target Beneficiaries
- Individual farmers
- Farmers' groups / FPOs / Panchayats / Cooperatives
- Water User Associations (WUAs)
- Rain-fed and off-grid farmers requiring irrigation power

Eligibility
Eligible
- Farmers owning agricultural land with required water source (for Component B standalone pumps).
- Farmers with existing grid-connected grid pump sets (for Component C solarization).
- Farmers/landowners with vacant, uncultivable, or barren land (for Component A solar plants).

Not Eligible
- Applicants without clear land title/RTC.
- Locations where grid connection is already available (strictly excluded under Component B standalone off-grid pumps).
- Farmers who have already availed solar pump subsidies from state energy departments.

Benefits
| Component | Benefit | Subsidies / Financial Structure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Component B | Standalone Solar Water Pumps (up to 7.5 HP) | 30% Central Financial Assistance (CFA) + 30% State Subsidy (Karnataka) + 30% NABARD/Bank Loan. Farmer pays only 10% of total cost. |
| Component C | Solarization of Grid-Connected Agriculture Pumps | 30% CFA + 30% State Subsidy + 40% Farmer share/Loan. Excess power generated sold back to DISCOM. |

Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card
- Land Record (RTC / Pahani)
- Certificate of Water Source / Borewell ownership
- Bank Account Passbook
- Electricity bill (for Component C grid-connected pump solarization)
- Passport size photograph

Application Process
1. Online Registration:
   - Visit official KREDL portal (kredl.karnataka.gov.in) or MNRE PM-KUSUM portal.
   - Fill out application for Component B (Standalone Pump) or Component C (Grid Solarization).
2. Document Upload & Payment:
   - Upload Aadhaar, RTC, Bank details, and borewell proof.
   - Pay 10% farmer share online to KREDL/DISCOM account.
3. Site Verification & Installation:
   - KREDL engineer inspects site for sunlight exposure and borewell depth.
   - Approved solar pump vendor installs solar panels, controller, and pump set.

Where to Apply
Online: https://pmkusum.mnre.gov.in | https://kredl.karnataka.gov.in
Offline: KREDL offices, Local DISCOM sub-division offices (BESCOM, HESCOM, etc.), Raitha Samparka Kendra.

Important Conditions
- Standalone solar pumps are allotted up to 7.5 HP capacity.
- Beware of fake websites; applications must only be made through official state energy portal (KREDL) or MNRE portal.

Farmer FAQs
Q1. How much does a farmer have to pay for a solar pump under PM-KUSUM Component B?
Answer: The farmer pays only 10% of the benchmark cost. 60% is covered by Central and State subsidies, and 30% can be funded via bank loan.

Q2. Can I get a solar pump if I already have a grid electricity connection?
Answer: Standalone pumps (Component B) are for off-grid areas. If you have grid power, you can apply under Component C to solarize your pump.

Q3. What is the maximum pump capacity supported for subsidy?
Answer: Solar pump capacities up to 7.5 HP are supported under the subsidy scheme.

Q4. Where to apply for PM-KUSUM in Karnataka?
Answer: Apply through the official KREDL portal (kredl.karnataka.gov.in) or your local DISCOM sub-division office.

Q5. Can I sell excess solar electricity generated on my farm?
Answer: Yes, under Component C and Component A, excess power generated can be sold back to local DISCOMs for extra monthly income.
