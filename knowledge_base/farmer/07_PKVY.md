# PKVY — Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana

Basic Information
Official Name: Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana
Abbreviation: PKVY
Government: Central / Karnataka State (60:40 share)
Ministry/Department: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / Department of Agriculture, Government of Karnataka
Implementing Agency: State Department of Agriculture / Organic Certification Agencies (Jaivik Kheti / PGA-India)
Current Status: Active (2026)
Official Website: https://pgsindia-ncof.gov.in | https://www.jaivikkheti.in

Purpose
PKVY promotes organic farming and sustainable soil health management through cluster-based organic production, Participatory Guarantee System (PGS) certification, processing, packaging, and direct marketing of organic produce.

Target Beneficiaries
- Small and marginal farmers
- Farmer clusters / Self-Help Groups / FPOs
- Rainfed and organic-oriented cultivators

Eligibility
Eligible
- Farmers willing to adopt organic farming practices in clusters of 20 or more hectares (50 or more farmers).
- Individual farmers joining recognized organic farming clusters in Karnataka.

Not Eligible
- Farmers practicing conventional chemical-heavy farming outside cluster systems without intent to convert.

Benefits
| Benefit | Amount / Details | Frequency / Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cluster Financial Assistance | Direct assistance of ₹50,000 per hectare over 3 years. (₹31,000/ha released directly to farmers via DBT for organic inputs like bio-fertilizers, vermicompost, neem cake, and seeds; balance for certification and marketing). | 3-year cycle per hectare |

Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card
- Land Record (RTC / Pahani)
- Bank Account Passbook
- FRUITS ID (FID)
- Cluster registration / PGS pledge document

Application Process
1. Cluster Formation: Form a group/cluster of at least 20–50 farmers owning contiguous land of 20 hectares in a village.
2. Group Registration: Register cluster with Assistant Director of Agriculture (ADA) at Taluk level or via Jaivik Kheti portal.
3. Adoption & Certification: Undergo training, adopt organic inputs, maintain PGS farm records, and receive PGS-India organic certification.

Where to Apply
Online: https://www.jaivikkheti.in | https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in
Offline: Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK), Office of Assistant Director of Agriculture (ADA).

Important Conditions
- Minimum cluster size of 20 hectares is required for full scheme benefit allocation.
- Chemical fertilizers and synthetic pesticides are strictly prohibited on enrolled plots.

Farmer FAQs
Q1. What is the total financial assistance provided under PKVY?
Answer: ₹50,000 per hectare over a period of 3 years, of which ₹31,000 is directly provided for organic inputs.

Q2. Can an individual farmer with 1 acre apply alone?
Answer: Individual farmers must join a village organic cluster (minimum 20 hectares total) to get PKVY benefits.

Q3. What certification is provided under PKVY?
Answer: Participatory Guarantee System (PGS-India) organic certification is provided free of cost to cluster members.

Q4. How is the subsidy money paid?
Answer: Input subsidies are transferred directly to the farmer’s Aadhaar-linked bank account via DBT.

Q5. Where can PKVY farmers sell their produce?
Answer: Farmers can register and sell produce directly on the official Jaivik Kheti e-commerce portal.
