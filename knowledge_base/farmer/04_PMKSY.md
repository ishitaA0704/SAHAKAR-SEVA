# PMKSY — Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana

Basic Information
Official Name: Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana
Abbreviation: PMKSY
Government: Central / Karnataka State (60:40 share)
Ministry/Department: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / Ministry of Jal Shakti / Karnataka Water Resources & Agriculture Departments
Implementing Agency: Department of Agriculture, Department of Horticulture, Karnataka
Current Status: Active (2026)
Official Website: https://pmksy.gov.in | https://raitamitra.karnataka.gov.in

Purpose
PMKSY aims to enhance physical access to water on farms, expand cultivable area under assured irrigation ("Har Khet Ko Pani"), improve on-farm water use efficiency through micro-irrigation ("Per Drop More Crop"), and support water conservation activities.

Target Beneficiaries
- Small and marginal farmers
- Rain-fed and drought-prone region farmers
- Women farmers
- SC/ST farmers
- Members of Water User Associations / FPOs

Eligibility
Eligible
- Farmers of all categories owning agricultural land with access to a water source (well, borewell, pond, canal).
- Members of cooperative societies, FPOs, and Self-Help Groups (SHGs).
- In Karnataka, preference and higher subsidies are accorded to SC/ST, small, and marginal farmers.

Not Eligible
- Farmers without identifiable land ownership or valid lease agreements.
- Land parcels that have already received micro-irrigation subsidies under the scheme within the last 7 to 10 years.

Benefits
| Benefit | Amount / Details | Frequency / Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Drip & Sprinkler Subsidy (Per Drop More Crop) | 55% subsidy for Small & Marginal farmers; 45% subsidy for Other category farmers under Central guidelines. (Karnataka state tops up subsidy up to 75%–90% for eligible small/marginal/SC/ST farmers). | One-time support per land parcel (up to 5 hectares limit) |

Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card
- Land ownership document (RTC / Pahani)
- FRUITS ID (FID)
- Bank Account Passbook
- Water source certificate / Borewell certificate or declaration
- Soil and water testing report (where applicable)

Application Process
1. Register on FRUITS Portal: Ensure farmer profile is updated on Karnataka FRUITS portal.
2. Apply online/offline:
   - Visit Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK) or apply via Karnataka Raitamitra portal / DBT Portal.
   - Select micro-irrigation component (Drip or Sprinkler).
   - Choose empaneled micro-irrigation vendor.
3. Field Inspection & Installation:
   - Assistant Agricultural Officer (AAO) inspects the field and approves estimation.
   - Authorized vendor installs micro-irrigation system.
4. Subsidy Disbursement:
   - Post-installation verification and GPS tagging completed.
   - Subsidy is released directly to vendor/farmer account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

Where to Apply
Online: https://pmksy.gov.in | https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in
Offline: Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK), Assistant Director of Agriculture (ADA) / Horticulture (ADH) offices, Grama One.

Important Conditions
- Installation must be carried out only through government-empaneled micro-irrigation vendors.
- Water source availability (borewell, open well, farm pond) is mandatory before applying for drip systems.

Farmer FAQs
Q1. What is the subsidy available for drip irrigation in Karnataka?
Answer: Small and marginal farmers receive up to 75%–90% subsidy in Karnataka by combining Central PMKSY and State top-up subsidies.

Q2. Who can apply for sprinkler irrigation units?
Answer: Any landholding farmer with a water source growing field crops like pulses, groundnut, or cereals can apply.

Q3. Can a farmer with 2 acres apply for micro-irrigation?
Answer: Yes, small farmers (holding up to 2 hectares / 5 acres) receive high priority and maximum subsidy benefits.

Q4. Where should I submit the application form in Karnataka?
Answer: You can apply online at the FRUITS/Raitamitra portal or submit physical documents at your nearest Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK).

Q5. Is a borewell mandatory to get drip irrigation subsidy?
Answer: A dedicated water source (borewell, open well, canal connection, or farm pond) is required.
