# PM-KISAN — Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi

Basic Information
Official Name: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi
Abbreviation: PM-KISAN
Government: Central
Ministry/Department: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
Implementing Agency: State Department of Agriculture / Revenue Department (Karnataka Agriculture Department)
Current Status: Active (2026)
Official Website: https://pmkisan.gov.in

Purpose
PM-KISAN provides direct income support to all landholding farmer families across India to help them meet financial needs related to agricultural inputs, crop health, domestic needs, and timely farm operations.

Target Beneficiaries
- Small farmers
- Marginal farmers
- Landholding farmer families with cultivable land in their names

Eligibility
Eligible
- Landholding farmer families who own cultivable land registered in state land records.
- Farmers registered on Karnataka FRUITS (Farmer Registration and Unified Beneficiary Information System) portal.
- Farmers with active Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts and completed e-KYC.

Not Eligible
- Institutional landholders.
- Farmer families where one or more members hold or held constitutional posts.
- Former and present Ministers, MPs, MLAs, Mayors, Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
- Serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government, PSUs, attached offices, and autonomous bodies (excluding Multi-Tasking Staff / Class IV / Group D employees).
- All superannuated / retired pensioners receiving a monthly pension of ₹10,000 or more.
- Persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
- Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with professional bodies.

Benefits
| Benefit | Amount / Details | Frequency / Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) | ₹6,000 per year | ₹2,000 paid in 3 equal installments every 4 months |

Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card of the farmer
- Land Ownership Record (Karnataka RTC / Pahani)
- FRUITS ID (FID) / Farmer Registration Number
- Bank Account Passbook (Aadhaar linked)
- Active Mobile Number

Application Process
1. Online Registration:
   - Visit official PM-KISAN portal (https://pmkisan.gov.in) or Karnataka FRUITS portal (https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in).
   - Click on 'New Farmer Registration'.
   - Select 'Rural' or 'Urban' farmer, enter Aadhaar number, state (Karnataka), and mobile number.
   - Verify OTP sent to mobile.
   - Enter land ownership details (Survey Number, Hissa Number, Sub-division, Khata/Pahani details).
   - Upload copy of RTC/Pahani and submit application.
2. Verification:
   - Application is verified by the local Village Accountant (VA) / Revenue Inspector / Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK) Agricultural Officer.
3. Disbursement:
   - Upon approval, ₹2,000 is directly transferred to the Aadhaar-linked bank account every quarter.

Where to Apply
Online: https://pmkisan.gov.in | https://fruits.karnataka.gov.in
Offline: Local Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK), Village Accountant (VA) Office, Grama One Center, or Common Service Centre (CSC)
Other authorized channels: CSC Digital Seva Kendras

Important Conditions
- e-KYC is mandatory (online via OTP on PM-KISAN portal or biometric at CSC).
- Bank account must be mapped with Aadhaar (Aadhaar Seeding and NPCI mapping required).
- Land records must be updated and cut-off date of landholding ownership is February 1, 2019 (except in inheritance cases).
- Names in Aadhaar, land record, and bank account must match.

Farmer FAQs
Q1. Can a farmer with 2 acres apply?
Answer: Yes. All landholding farmers, regardless of land size, are eligible if they own cultivable land and do not fall under exclusion categories.

Q2. Do I need Aadhaar for PM-KISAN?
Answer: Yes, Aadhaar is mandatory for registration, verification, and payment processing through Aadhaar-Enabled Payment System (AEPS).

Q3. What documents are required?
Answer: Aadhaar Card, RTC/Pahani land record, Bank Passbook, and active Mobile Number.

Q4. How much money will I receive?
Answer: You will receive ₹6,000 per year, disbursed as ₹2,000 every 4 months directly to your bank account.

Q5. Where can I apply in Karnataka?
Answer: You can apply online at pmkisan.gov.in, fruits.karnataka.gov.in, or visit your nearest Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK) or Grama One center.
