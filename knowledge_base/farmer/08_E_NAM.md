# e-NAM — National Agriculture Market

Basic Information
Official Name: National Agriculture Market
Abbreviation: e-NAM
Government: Central / Karnataka State (Integrated with ReMS / Rashtriya e-Market Services)
Ministry/Department: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / Department of Agricultural Marketing, Karnataka
Implementing Agency: Small Farmers Agribusiness Consortium (SFAC) & Karnataka Agricultural Produce Market Committees (APMC / ReMS)
Current Status: Active (2026)
Official Website: https://enam.gov.in | https://remsltd.in

Purpose
e-NAM creates a unified national electronic trading market for agricultural commodities by networking existing APMC mandis, ensuring transparent price discovery, competitive bidding, direct online payment, and broader market access for farmers across states.

Target Beneficiaries
- All landholding and landless farmers
- FPOs / Farmer Producer Companies
- Traders, commission agents, and processors

Eligibility
Eligible
- Any farmer producing agricultural commodities in India/Karnataka.
- Registered APMC sellers / traders.

Not Eligible
- None. Open to all agricultural producers.

Benefits
| Benefit | Amount / Details | Frequency / Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pan-India Electronic Trading | Transparent quality-based assaying, competitive online bidding by buyers nationwide, removal of middleman exploitation, direct online bank settlement. | Per transaction / Sale batch |

Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card
- Bank Account Passbook
- Mobile Number
- APMC Farmer Registration / FRUITS ID (in Karnataka)

Application Process
1. Online Registration:
   - Visit e-NAM portal (enam.gov.in) or ReMS portal (remsltd.in).
   - Click 'Register as Farmer', enter Aadhaar, mobile number, and bank details.
2. Mandi Integration:
   - Bring produce to e-NAM / ReMS enabled APMC mandi.
   - Sample is tested at assaying lab, lot is uploaded online for national bidding.
3. Sale & Payment:
   - Highest online bid is accepted by farmer; sale value is credited directly to farmer's bank account.

Where to Apply
Online: https://enam.gov.in | https://remsltd.in
Offline: APMC Mandi counters, Unified Market Platform (UMP) desks, Raitha Samparka Kendra.

Important Conditions
- Commodity quality assaying is mandatory before lot listing.
- Bank account details must be verified for direct online settlement.

Farmer FAQs
Q1. What is e-NAM?
Answer: e-NAM is an online trading platform that connects APMC mandis across India, allowing farmers to sell produce to buyers anywhere in the country.

Q2. Is there any registration fee for farmers on e-NAM?
Answer: No, farmer registration on e-NAM and ReMS portals is completely free.

Q3. How does a farmer get paid after selling on e-NAM?
Answer: Payment is transferred directly into the farmer's registered bank account electronically on the same day.

Q4. How does e-NAM function in Karnataka?
Answer: In Karnataka, e-NAM works in integration with the state's Unified Market Platform (ReMS / UMP) across APMC mandis.

Q5. Can FPOs trade on e-NAM?
Answer: Yes, FPOs can register as aggregators and sell member produce directly on e-NAM.
