# Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Basic Information
Official Name: Kisan Credit Card Scheme
Abbreviation: KCC
Government: Central
Ministry/Department: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance
Implementing Agency: Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks (RRBs like KVGB, PKGB), State Cooperative Banks
Current Status: Active (2026)
Official Website: https://agricoop.gov.in | https://pmkisan.gov.in (KCC module)

Purpose
KCC ensures that farmers receive timely and adequate credit from the banking system to cover short-term credit requirements for crop cultivation, post-harvest expenses, farm maintenance, household needs, and allied activities like animal husbandry and fisheries.

Target Beneficiaries
- Small farmers
- Marginal farmers
- Owner cultivators
- Tenant farmers, sharecroppers, and oral lessees
- Self-Help Groups (SHGs) or Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) of farmers
- Animal husbandry and fisheries farmers

Eligibility
Eligible
- All individual/joint borrowers who are owner-cultivators.
- Tenant farmers, oral lessees, and sharecroppers with valid lease agreements or self-declarations.
- Farmers involved in animal husbandry, poultry, dairy, or fisheries.

Not Eligible
- Non-farmers or individuals without involvement in agriculture or allied activities.
- Defaulted borrowers with un-cleared Non-Performing Assets (NPAs) at commercial or cooperative banks.

Benefits
| Benefit | Amount / Details | Frequency / Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Revolving Credit Limit | Credit limit fixed based on crop pattern, scale of finance, and land holding. Collateral-free loan up to ₹1.60 lakh (extended to ₹3.00 lakh in select cases). | Valid for 5 years with annual review |
| Interest Subvention | Concessional interest rate of 7% per annum. Additional 3% prompt repayment incentive (PRI) reduces effective interest rate to 4% per annum for loans up to ₹3.00 lakh. | Annual |

Required Documents
- Duly filled KCC application form
- Aadhaar Card and PAN Card (or Form 60)
- Land ownership document (RTC / Pahani)
- Crop pattern and area details
- Bank Account details
- Passport size photographs

Application Process
1. Obtain Form: Download KCC application form from PM-KISAN portal or collect from any commercial bank / RRB / Cooperative bank branch.
2. Fill & Attach: Fill in land, crop, and personal details. Attach copy of Aadhaar, RTC, and bank passbook.
3. Submit: Submit form at local bank branch or Raitha Samparka Kendra.
4. Sanction: Bank verifies RTC land details and sanctions credit limit, issuing a KCC ATM cum Debit Card.

Where to Apply
Online: https://pmkisan.gov.in (KCC online submission module) | Bank NetBanking portals
Offline: Public Sector Banks, Regional Rural Banks (Pragathi Krishna / Karnataka Vikas Grameena Bank), Cooperative Banks, District Central Cooperative (DCC) Banks.

Important Conditions
- Card validity is 5 years, subject to annual review and extension.
- Prompt repayment within 12 months is mandatory to claim the 3% interest subvention incentive.
- Must be linked to Aadhaar for interest subvention disbursement.

Farmer FAQs
Q1. Can a farmer with 2 acres get a Kisan Credit Card?
Answer: Yes. Landholding size does not restrict eligibility; the credit limit will be calculated based on your land area and crops grown.

Q2. What is the interest rate on a KCC loan?
Answer: The standard interest rate is 7% per annum. If you repay on time, you get a 3% subsidy, making the effective interest rate just 4%.

Q3. Is collateral required for a KCC loan up to ₹1.6 lakh?
Answer: No. Agricultural loans up to ₹1.60 lakh are completely collateral-free.

Q4. Can dairy or poultry farmers apply for KCC?
Answer: Yes. KCC benefits have been extended to animal husbandry, dairy, poultry, and fisheries farmers.

Q5. How long is the KCC card valid?
Answer: The KCC limit is valid for 5 years, subject to an annual performance review by the bank.
